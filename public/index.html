    Questions

    Straight answers

    Can I use CareerPilot AI today?

    We are opening access in stages. Join the early access list and tell us whether you are a student, recruiter or college, and we will contact you as access opens for your group.

    What will it cost?

    Pricing has not been announced yet.

    Is the match percentage a prediction that I will get the job?

    No. It compares your skills and preferences with what a role asks for, and shows you the reasons. It is a progress indicator, not a forecast of hiring or placement.

    Is the resume score an ATS score?

    No. The resume analyser gives guidance and a checklist. It is not an official applicant tracking system score and does not guarantee selection. You choose which suggestions to apply.

    Does AI decide who gets hired?

    No. Matching helps recruiters compare candidates on job-related criteria. Recruiters make the decisions, and nothing is rejected automatically.

    Who can see my resume and profile?

    Recruiters can open your profile and resume for roles you apply to, and administrators see only what their role allows. Resumes are stored privately and are not available through public links.

    Can I delete my resume or my account?

    You can replace or delete your resume at any time. You can also ask us to delete your account and records through the contact form below.

    How are recruiters verified?

    Recruiters submit company name, official email, website and contact details. The college or admin team reviews them before the verified label appears.
